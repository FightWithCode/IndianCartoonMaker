# The village well | chowk | wide | day
[Meera enters the banyan square. Hari is looking at the old well.]
Narrator: Every afternoon, Meera took the long way home past the village well.

# A very important bucket | chowk | medium | day
[Hari points to the well, worried. Meera listens with her hand on her chin.]
Hari {worried}: Meera, the bucket is gone! How will we draw water now?
Meera {happy}: Kaka, look behind you. It is right by your foot.

# The culprit | chowk | close | day
[Hari looks down at the basket, then laughs. Add a funny sound.]
Hari {funny}: Arre! My eyes must have gone for a tea break.

# Walking home | chowk | wide | dusk
[The two wave goodbye in the square at dusk.]
Narrator: With a laugh, they carried the water home before sunset.
